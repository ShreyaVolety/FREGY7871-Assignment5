Generative AI was used as a coding, debugging, and writing assistant during this project. It helped generate and troubleshoot Python code for processing Apify Twitter exports, collecting news through GDELT and BigQuery, implementing BERTopic, NMF, and LDA, transformer-based sentiment scoring, retrieving Polymarket probabilities and equity prices, and producing tables and visualizations. It also assisted with calculating contemporaneous and lagged correlations, applying Holm corrections for multiple testing, and constructing an equal-weight equity basket with returns adjusted against SPY. I ran the code, investigated errors, and incorporated revisions into the analysis.
I iterated on the data collection and analysis as the results became available. This included merging and deduplicating tweet exports, piloting a BART-based relevance classifier, adopting a lightweight tweet filter when the pilot proved too computationally expensive, and refitting the three topic models on the retained data. AI assisted with reviewing topic keywords and examples and proposing issue labels. When the original news clusters provided uneven daily coverage, it helped implement a supplementary topic-specific headline collection and rebuild the sentiment indices. AI also proposed policy-exposed equities and supporting company filings for the basket. I reviewed the outputs and selected which analyses and exhibits to include in the final report.
AI also helped interpret tables and figures and draft and revise passages describing the data, methodology, findings, and limitations. I assembled the report and reviewed the proposed interpretations against the empirical outputs. The conclusions distinguish issue prominence within the collected samples from representative voter preferences, emotional tone from partisan support, and exploratory correlations from demonstrated predictive or causal relationships. They also distinguish pre-election equity performance from the realized effects of election results and account for the lack of statistical significance after multiple-testing correction. I retain responsibility for the final methodological choices, interpretation, and submitted report.
All relevant functions and analysis code are contained in the notebook rather than organized into a separate project structure. The complete datasets have not been pushed to the repository due to storage constraints.
